# Inbox

Drop your raw material here for Claude to turn into the site. Nothing in this folder
appears on the website.

Good things to put here:

- Your resume (PDF, or pasted into a `.txt` or `.md` file)
- CAD files: STL, STEP, or OBJ
- Photos of your hardware, test setups, and results
- Screenshots or exports from Notion
- A notes file per project, even rough bullet points: what it was, your role, dates,
  what you decided and why, results with numbers

One folder per project helps, e.g. `inbox/rover-suspension/`.

To upload on github.com: open this folder, click **Add file**, then **Upload files**,
and drag files in. Files over 25 MB can't be uploaded through the website. For big
CAD files, export a coarser STL, or zip it.
